Features:

  • Increases Chassis Rigidity and Reduces Flex: During cornering or when encountering road imperfections, your car's chassis can subtly twist. The strut towers, where the top of your suspension mounts, bear a significant load. A strut tower bar connects these left and right strut towers, reinforcing this area and minimizing chassis flex. This leads to improved steering response and more stable cornering.
  • Enhances Handling and Steering Responsiveness: With increased chassis rigidity, your vehicle reacts more directly and precisely to steering inputs. Play in the steering is reduced, and unnecessary body movements are suppressed, making it easier for you to guide the car exactly where you want it to go.
  • Optimizes Suspension Performance: A strut tower bar helps your suspension work at its best. By minimizing the movement of the strut towers during suspension operation, it allows the springs and dampers to absorb impacts and maintain tire contact with the road more efficiently, as they were designed to. This can even have a positive impact on ride comfort.
  • Improves Tire Grip: The combined effect of increased chassis rigidity and optimized suspension performance ultimately leads to better tire grip. With less unnecessary body movement, your tires can maintain more consistent and stable contact with the road surface, which contributes to improved braking performance and higher cornering limits.
