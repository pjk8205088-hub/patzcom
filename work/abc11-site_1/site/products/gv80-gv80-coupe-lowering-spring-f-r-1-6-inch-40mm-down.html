Technical Specifications & Durability (Optimized for All Sedan Models)

- Custom-tailored for GVWR: These springs are precision-engineered to meet or exceed the vehicle's Gross Vehicle Weight Rating (GVWR), typically supporting over 6,000 lbs (2,720kg) for most SUV's applications. They provide superior load support compared to OEM springs, ensuring maximum stability even under full passenger or cargo loads.
- High-Carbon SAE9254 Steel: 100% Manufactured in South Korea using premium cold-rolled SAE9254 high-tensile steel. Our springs undergo rigorous stress testing, guaranteeing over 500,000 cycles of durability without compromising performance.
- Anti-Sagging Guarantee: Utilizing advanced Cold-Forming Technology, Storm Springs offers exceptional resistance to "sagging" (permanent deformation). Unlike cheaper hot-rolled alternatives, our springs maintain their original ride height and performance for years to come.

Lowering Springs Crafted by Suspension Experts

Experience perfect performance and stability with our lowering springs, featuring optimized settings for your original suspension. The spring material is 100% KDM high-grade cold-wound material for automobiles, which is lighter and more durable than the original.

Features:

  • Engineered and developed by experienced suspension specialists
  • Lighter than the stock springs but also offers significantly improved durability
  • Offers reduced weight compared to stock springs while providing superior durability
  • Enhanced performance and reliable stability, improved handling, a stable ride
  • Perfectly tuned settings for seamless integration with factory suspension

Reviews:

  • If you install the Storm lowering spring on the GV80, the ride quality may be somewhat firmer compared to the original. However, some reviews say it maintains a ride quality close to the original, so it may feel different depending on your driving style or sensitivity.
  • Some reviews say, "It is a down spring that maintains a soft ride quality like the original without compromising the ride quality with appropriate downforce."
  • However, the lowered spring is likely to feel stiffer than the original. This is because the suspension stroke decreases and the spring stiffness increases as the ride height decreases. In particular, the shock may be felt more than the original when passing over bumps or speed bumps.
