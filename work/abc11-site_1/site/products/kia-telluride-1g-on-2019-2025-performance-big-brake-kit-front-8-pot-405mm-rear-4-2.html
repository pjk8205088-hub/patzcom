[Required Installation Guide for Rear Kit]

According to the STOLZ factory’s official installation guidelines, there may be a minor clearance requirement (approx. 1–2mm) where the bracket meets the vehicle's knuckle.

Please Note: If interference occurs, the modification must be made to the knuckle itself, not the bracket. Professional mechanics simply need to lightly grind the specific contact point on the knuckle to ensure a perfect fit.

This is not a product defect. As this is a high-performance aftermarket system, this minor trimming is a standard part of the installation process for the Palisade (and similar models, such as the Telluride) in Korea. This procedure does not compromise the vehicle's safety or structural integrity. This fitment method has been used successfully since the vehicle's release with zero reported issues. We recommend professional installation to ensure the best performance.
