The BIGFOOT CONTOUR Coilover Kit is meticulously engineered with a perfect match between structural vehicle characteristics and damper profiles. Moving away from conventional setups that chase high spring stiffness alone, the CONTOUR system focuses on achieving the ideal balance tailored to the specific load distribution and driving dynamics of each vehicle generation and model.
