Reviews:

  • When we measured the SANTA FE (DM) stock springs, considering the change in generation, the front wheels have only slightly increased in stiffness compared to the previous model, while the rear wheels have increased in stiffness and height significantly.
  • Fortunately, I was able to find a spec that would fit. Storm Lowering Springss, above all, does not harm the characteristics of the original electronic shock absorber, which is known for its good ride quality.
  • If you install the Storm lowering spring on the SANTA FE (DM), the ride quality may be somewhat firmer compared to the original. However, some reviews say it maintains a ride quality close to the original, so it may feel different depending on your driving style or sensitivity.
  • Some reviews say, "It is a down spring that maintains a soft ride quality like the original without compromising the ride quality with appropriate downforce."
  • However, the down spring is likely to feel stiffer than the original. This is because the suspension stroke decreases and the spring stiffness increases as the ride height decreases. In particular, the shock may be felt more than the original when passing over bumps or speed bumps.
