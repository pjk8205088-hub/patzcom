User Experience (UX) / Customer Experience (CX)

HARDRON ZR Pads on IONIQ 6N
I bought the ZR pads late last year and fitted them in February. I’ve done about 2,000km road driving on them and they’ve done 1.5 track days in the last week. My initial road driving impressions are elsewhere in this group. On the track, they were very impressive. I was on a 3.2km fast track (Queensland Raceway). It has 2x 180-60kph stops, 1x 215-60kph stop and a 200-130kph corner. I was completing about 15 laps in a single 20min session and did about 5 sessions over the day. These pads performed every single time. I had complete confidence and was braking later and harder than my 1.7T S550 Mustang on stock pads and factory Brembos. The modulation remained constant throughout the day, the pedal feel was consistent and only on the last lap of the day did I think that the braking system was starting to complain. (However, I now suspect that was the rear motor getting hot and pulling the regen rather than the pads). On the BIG stops I could go deep into the braking and modulate the application. Activating the ABS was straightforward and I was able to work with the system. I am pretty happy with them and have no issues running them on my car. The fact that they make a matching set for front and rear, whilst Hyundai only make front pads, is also a bonus. 

Note: I have also flushed the factory brake fluid for Bremtech F2R but I am still running the factory 21” wheels and tyres.
