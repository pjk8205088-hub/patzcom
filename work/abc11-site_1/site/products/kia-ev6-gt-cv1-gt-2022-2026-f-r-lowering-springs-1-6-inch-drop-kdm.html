Reviews:

  • If you install the Storm lowering spring on the EV6, the ride quality may be somewhat firmer compared to the original. However, some reviews say it maintains a ride quality close to the original, so it may feel different depending on your driving style or sensitivity.

  • However, the lowered spring is likely to feel stiffer than the original. This is because the suspension stroke decreases and the spring stiffness increases as the ride height decreases. In particular, the shock may be felt more than the original when passing over bumps or speed bumps.
