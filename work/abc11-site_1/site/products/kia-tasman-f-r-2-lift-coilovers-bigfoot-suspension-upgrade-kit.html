Why Choose BIGFOOT Suspension? 

BIGFOOT is a premium heavy-duty suspension brand engineered by Korea's leading suspension specialist (an official development partner of Mando). Trusted for over two decades in extreme off-road, towing, and armored vehicle applications.

Key Features & Performance Advantages:

  • Ultimate Anti-Sagging & Superior Towing Performance (Heavy-Duty Performance)
    - BIGFOOT is widely recognized as the ultimate solution for eliminating rear-end sag. It is the go-to choice among pickup truck owners, caravan users, and heavy-payload haulers. Even when towing heavy trailers or carrying maximum cargo, BIGFOOT suspension delivers exceptional restoring force and structural durability, maintaining the optimal ride height and stability at a world-class level.
  • Seamless OEM Compatibility & Refined Ride Comfort (Ride Quality)
    - Unlike low-end lift kits that cause a harsh, bouncy ride and driver fatigue, BIGFOOT is precision-engineered to perfectly match the vehicle's original geometry and damper damping characteristics. It delivers the perfect balance—providing a substantial lift while effectively suppressing body roll and maintaining a firm, well-composed, and comfortable ride during daily driving.
  • Unrivaled Material Reliability in Extreme Environments (Durability)
    - BIGFOOT's material technology is proven under the most grueling conditions, supplying OEM springs for specialized high-load applications such as armored military vehicles, heavy-duty tow trucks, and utility vehicles. Utilizing 100% premium domestic cold-wound steel (specifically high-tensile SAE 9254), these springs are virtually immune to sagging or fatigue-induced settling, even after years of hard core off-roading. This exceptional longevity is why professional tuning shops and international buyers highly trust BIGFOOT.
