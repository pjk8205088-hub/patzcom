- Progressive Design vs. Linear Design:
You may notice these are not linear springs. In the case of the Carnival KA4, the spring's outer diameter is quite large while the free length is short and the number of coils (turns) is limited. These physical constraints make a Linear Design impractical for performance. Instead, we utilize a Progressive (Variable Rate) Design. This allows the spring to remain compliant for a smooth ride during daily driving, while rapidly increasing resistance as the load increases to prevent bottoming out.

- Key Engineering Focus
Front Suspension: As shown in the data, the front isn't just about "stiffness." We focused on material and design changes to achieve both a lift and a significant improvement in ride comfort.

Anti-Sagging Performance: The core strength of Bigfoot springs is their long-term Anti-Sagging durability. They are engineered to maintain their height even under constant heavy loads over many years.
