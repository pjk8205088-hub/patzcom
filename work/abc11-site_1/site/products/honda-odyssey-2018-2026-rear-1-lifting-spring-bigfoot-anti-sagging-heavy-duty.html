To provide you with a clearer understanding of our product..,

Here are the technical specifications and the engineering philosophy behind the Bigfoot springs for the Odyssey RL6.

- The following data was obtained using our in-house testing equipment. Please note that these physical measurements typically reflect values about 10% lower than theoretical formulas. However, since the OEM springs were tested under the exact same conditions, you can use these for a direct relative comparison.

- Progressive Design vs. Linear Design:
You may notice these are not linear springs. In the case of the Odyssey RL6, the spring's outer diameter is quite large while the free length is short and the number of coils (turns) is limited. These physical constraints make a Linear Design impractical for performance. Instead, we utilize a Progressive (Variable Rate) Design. This allows the spring to remain compliant for a smooth ride during daily driving, while rapidly increasing resistance as the load increases to prevent bottoming out.

- Key Engineering Focus
Front Suspension: As shown in the data, the front isn't just about "stiffness." We focused on material and design changes to achieve both a lift and a significant improvement in ride comfort.

Anti-Sagging Performance: The core strength of Bigfoot springs is their long-term Anti-Sagging durability. They are engineered to maintain their height even under constant heavy loads over many years.

Why Korean Users Choose Bigfoot: In Korea, even owners who don't carry heavy loads consider these springs essential. They eliminate the "floaty" feel of the stock suspension, providing a much more planted and stable driving experience.

We are confident that once you install these, you will feel the difference in stability. We look forward to your feedback.
