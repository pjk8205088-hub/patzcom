About Us patzcom

patzcom is a performance parts store specialising in lift kits, coilovers, lowering springs, big brake kits, brake pads and chassis reinforcement for modern SUVs and passenger cars.

PATZCOM is operated by a Korea-based seller and development team for U.S. customers who want direct-fit Korean performance parts with clear English product information, U.S.-friendly checkout, and tracked international shipping.

Payment setup for U.S. buyers

  • Primary payment option: PayPal checkout.
  • Secondary payment option: Stripe card checkout for Visa, Mastercard, and AMEX, planned through our U.S. Stripe Atlas setup.
  • We do not store card numbers on PATZCOM servers. Payment details are handled by the payment provider.

We carry 5 in-house brands - BIGFOOT, HARDRON ZR, LUXON, STOLZ BRAKE, and STORMSPRING - across 39 direct-fit products, engineered and tested for specific chassis codes rather than universal guesswork.
